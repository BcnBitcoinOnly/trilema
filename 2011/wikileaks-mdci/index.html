1. Effective February 14, 2010, unless otherwise noted, allowances and/or post differential rates are revised as shown. These changes are issued under TL:SR-727.

2. Please inform local offices of all U.S. government agencies at the posts listed in this cable.

3. Important: Read this entire message as posts may be listed more than once. Changes are listed by allowance or differential rather than by post.

4. Taiwan has been included, when applicable, under arrangement with the American Institute in Taiwan.

5. The Living Quarters Allowance changes are based on a quarterly adjustment to reflect exchange rate changes.

6. All Post Allowance changes are based on currency exchange rate information reported to the Office of Allowances as of the first Thursday of Pay Period 3 (February 4, 2010).

7. The Education Allowance rate changes are based on updated information provided by post for the current school year.

8. The reporting requirements for submission of the Post (Hardship) Differential Surveys for Conakry, Guinea and Manila, Philippines are amended effective February 14, 2010, with TL:SR-727 as follows:
