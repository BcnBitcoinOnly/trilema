SIPDIS, PASS TO ALL USAID MISSIONS

E.O. 12958: N/A TAGS: ALOW, AMGT, APER, AFIN SUBJECT: Allowance and Post Differential Reclassifications TL:SR-709

Ref: Section 920 Standardized Regulations (GC,FA)

1. Effective June 7, 2009, unless otherwise noted, allowances and/or post differential rates are revised as shown. These changes are issued under TL:SR-709.

2. Please inform local offices of all U.S. government agencies at the posts listed in this cable.

3. Important: Read this entire message as posts may be listed more than once. Changes are listed by allowance or differential rather than by post.

4. Taiwan has been included, when applicable, under arrangement with the American Institute in Taiwan.

5. The changes in the Danger Pay Allowance for the Central African Republic and Chad are based on updated Danger Pay Factors Form submitted by Bangui and Ndjamena, respectively.

6. The Post (Hardship) Differential changes for the Central African Republic and the Bahamas are based on updated Post (Hardship) Differential surveys submitted by Bangui and the Department of the Navy, respectively.

7. The changes in the Living Quarters Allowance rates for Bangkok, Thailand, are based on living quarters expenditure information provided by Bangkok.

8. The Post Allowance changes for Denmark, Sudan, and Turkey are based on surveys submitted by Copenhagen, Khartoum, and Istanbul, respectively. All other Post Allowance changes are based on currency exchange rate information reported to the Office of Allowances as of the first Thursday of Pay Period 11 (May 28, 2009).

9. The Education ?At-Post? Allowance rate changes for Colombo and Port of Spain are based on new information supplied by posts for the 2008-2009 school year. All other Education Allowance rate changes are for the 2009-2010 school year.

10. This information is available on the State Department internet and intranet sites. The internet site address is: http://aoprals.state.gov. The intranet (internal State Department network) site address is: http://aoprals.a.state.gov/.
