Ref: Section 920 Standardized Regulations (GC,FA)

1. Effective September 27, 2009, unless otherwise noted, allowances and/or post differential rates are revised as shown. These changes are issued under TL:SR-717.

2. Please inform local offices of all U.S. government agencies at the posts listed in this cable.

3. Important: Read this entire message as posts may be listed more than once. Changes are listed by allowance or differential rather than by post.

4. Taiwan has been included, when applicable, under arrangement with the American Institute in Taiwan.

5. The Post (Hardship) Differential changes for Niger are based on a Post (Hardship) Differential survey submitted by Niamey.

6. The Living Quarters Allowance changes for Korea are based on updated living quarters expenditure data submitted by the Department of the Army and DoDEA.

7. The Post Allowance changes for Sao Paulo, Brazil; and the United Kingdom are based on updated Retail Price Surveys submitted by Sao Paulo and Croughton, respectively. All other Post Allowance changes are based on currency exchange rate information reported to the Office of Allowances as of the first Thursday of Pay Period 19 (September 17, 2009).

8. The Education At-Post Allowance rate changes are based on updated information provided by posts for the 2009-2010 school year.

9. The reporting requirements for submission of the Retail Price Survey for Croughton and Oxfordshire, United Kingdom, in DSSR 920 are amended effective September 27, 2009, with TL:SR-717 as follows:
