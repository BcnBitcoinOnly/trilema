E.O. 12958: N/A TAGS: ALOW, AMGT, APER, AFIN SUBJECT: Allowance and Post Differential Reclassifications TL:SR-715

Ref: Section 920 Standardized Regulations (GC,FA)

1. Effective August 30, 2009, unless otherwise noted, allowances and/or post differential rates are revised as shown. These changes are issued under TL:SR-715.

2. Please inform local offices of all U.S. government agencies at the posts listed in this cable.

3. Important: Read this entire message as posts may be listed more than once. Changes are listed by allowance or differential rather than by post.

4. Taiwan has been included, when applicable, under arrangement with the American Institute in Taiwan.

5. The Post (Hardship) Differential changes for Ghana and Guatemala are based on Post (Hardship) Differential surveys submitted by Accra and Guatemala City, respectively.

6. The changes in the Living Quarters Allowance rates for Canada, Chile, Colombia, and Israel are based on updated living quarters expenditure surveys.

7. The Post Allowance changes for Belgium, Jamaica, Lesotho, Oman, and Zambia are based on updated Retail Price Surveys submitted by Brussels, Kingston, Maseru, Muscat, and Lusaka, respectively. All other Post Allowance changes are based on currency exchange rate information reported to the Office of Allowances as of the first Thursday of Pay Period 17 (August 20, 2009).

8. The Education "At-Post" Allowance rate changes are based on updated information provided by posts for the 2009-2010 school year.

9. Effective August 30, 2009, with TL:SR-715, sections 030, 040, 070, 120, 130, 220, 240, 250, 260, 270, 280, 600 and 960 of the Department of State Standardized Regulations (DSSR) are amended in final publication. The majority of these changes provide for agencies to include domestic partners of U.S. government employees as family members for
