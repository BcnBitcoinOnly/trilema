Cel mai bun film - centralizator

Sunday, 30 January, Year 3 d.Tr. | Author: Mircea Popescu

Acum vreo cam circa aproximativ 48 de ore plus minus niste minute imi traznea prin cap chestia cu filmul cel mai obscur.

Spre incintarea mea ideea a prins, probabil pentru ca - in termenii lui Ste - "Bun, ideea mi se pare complet retardata, imi place."

Sa trecem rezultatele in revista :

Cristina Stanciu :

    They Live by Night, Offret, Mephisto, Lantana, Hiroshima Mon Amour, Prospero’s Books, Le violon rouge, Amarcord, Le journal d’une femme de chambre, La double vie de Veronique.

Raluca Avramescu :

    Nói Albínói, Pin… A Plastic Nightmare, Cronos, Ta’m e guilass, Ratcatcher, Kyvadlo, jáma a nadeje, The God, Záhrada, Amnesia, Senki.

Diana Coman (o lista mai scurtuta asa) :

    Тот самый Мюнхгаузен, Le Placard, Vidocq.

Richard Marius Ilie :

    Drawing Restraint 9, Crying out love, in the center of the world, Eden Log, Cypher, Tokyo!, A l'interieur, Santa Sangre, Caravaggio, Los Amantes del Circulo Polar, Les Yeux Sans Visage.

Alin Gogan :

    El verdugo, Nanjing Nanjing, Angel face, Sans toit ni loi, Beneath Hill 60, L’heure d’été, Last Holiday, Max Manus, A Face in the Crowd, Lust for Life

Anca Hotineanu :

    L’amant, Feast of love, La Leggenda del pianista sull’oceano, Les femmes de l’ombre, North Country, Nell, The air I breathe, The Jane Austen book club, When Nietzsche Wept, Stranger than fiction.

Madalin Blidaru :

    An old fashioned thanksgiving, An American crime, Blood and bone, Catch me if you can, Legend of Earthsea, Pari e dispari, Bulgare de huma, Before the light turns green, Last exit, Wall of secrets.

Florin Cristian :

    Der Blaue Engel, Stranger on the Third Floor, The Curse of the Cat People, Blood and Black Lace, Seul Contre Tous, Onibaba, Navajo Joe, Night of the Lepus, Five Fingers of Death.

Marian Iuga :

    House of D., Slipstream, The Ron Clark Story, Saints and soldiers, The Ungodly, The Last Winter, Somers Town, Grace is gone, Casshern, Valerie tyden divu.

Adina Chitu :

    Amphetamine, Les filles du botaniste, Nae maeumui punggeum/ the harmonium in my memory, The Holy mountain, Welkkeom tu Dongmakgol, Ssang-hwa-jeom/ A frozen flower, Life of Brian, Sam gang yi/ Three...extremes, Symbol, Babettes gæstebud.

Mircea Dumitrescu

    La chiave, Paprika, Mon Amour, Trasgredire, Il diavolo in corpo, Il fiore delle mille e una notte, Il tè nel deserto, The duellists, The hunger, Ensayo de un Crimen.

Adrian Mihalcioiu

    Inu to watashi no jû no yakusoku, Tôkyô zankoku keisatsu, Tsuribaka Nisshi 19, Kataude mashin gâru.

Doua zile, o suta si ceva de filme din care fac pariu ca n-ati vazut jumatate. Puterea internetului.

Congrats to the winners.
