REF: STATE 80344

1. This is an action request. Please see paragraphs 7-9.
----------
BACKGROUND
----------
2. Background: As described in reftel, on May 1, 2007 Secretary Rice announced that the State Department will

SIPDIS host the Washington International Renewable Energy Conference (WIREC 2008) in March 2008. WIREC is being implemented in partnership with the American Council on Renewable Energy (ACORE), the United States Energy Association (USEA), multiple USG agencies and the White House (DOE, USDA, EPA, etc.). WIREC 2008 is the third global ministerial-level event on renewable energy (Bonn 2004, Beijing 2005). WIREC 2008 is an opportunity to advance the Administration's priority of responding to the

SUBJECT: REQUEST FOR DEMARCHE ON MINISTERIAL DELEGATIONS TO WASHINGTON INTERNATIONAL RENEWABLE ENERGY CONFERENCE (WIREC 200 challenges of sustainable economic growth and climate change through renewable energy. WIREC also serves as a global platform for ministers and senior level leaders from the private sector, sub-national governments and civil society to advance the integration of renewable energy in their countries. Planning for WIREC 2008 has now advanced to the stage that the Department wishes to solidify the invitation list for the Ministerial in hopes of sending formal invitations to members of Official WIREC Ministerial Delegations before the end of October.

3. We envision the three-day WIREC Government Meetings will include speeches by a minimum of three U.S. Cabinet Secretaries, and possibly President Bush. We expect approximately 2,000 delegates to participate in the Government meetings at the Washington Convention Center. Anticipated breakdown of those 2,000 at this point, five and one-half months out is:

- 750 international government attendees consisting of 75 Ministerial-level delegations of up to 10 per country - 500 USG attendees, including federal, state, and local Officials - 750 international and U.S. private sector delegates

4. Official Individual Country Ministerial-level delegations may consist of a maximum of 10 individuals per country, composed of up to three Ministers, including the Ministers of Foreign Affairs, Energy, Agriculture, or Environment (or the equivalent Ministers covering those portfolios) and three subnational government representatives. The European Commission is also invited to send a delegation of 10 members. We defer to each country's Minister of Foreign Affairs (MFA) to determine their country's precise makeup of the 'Official WIREC Ministerial Delegation,' as well as to inform us of their 'Head of Delegation,' assuming more than one Minister-level delegate is part of that country's official delegation. However, we appreciate Embassy engagement with the MFA to ensure that the delegation is comprised of appropriate Ministers and subnational government representatives.

5.
