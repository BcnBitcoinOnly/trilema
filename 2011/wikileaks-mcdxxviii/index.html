Ref: Section 920 Standardized Regulations (GC,FA)

1. Effective August 16, 2009, unless otherwise noted, allowances and/or post differential rates are revised as shown. These changes are issued under TL:SR-714.

2. Please inform local offices of all U.S. government agencies at the posts listed in this cable.

3. Important: Read this entire message as posts may be listed more than once. Changes are listed by allowance or differential rather than by post.

4. Taiwan has been included, when applicable, under arrangement with the American Institute in Taiwan.

5. The Post (Hardship) Differential changes for Greece are based on a Post (Hardship) Differential survey submitted by Athens.

6. The changes in the Living Quarters Allowance rates for Australia, Norway, and Portugal are based on living quarters expenditure surveys submitted by Air Force.

7. All Post Allowance changes are based on currency exchange rate information reported to the Office of Allowances as of the first Thursday of Pay Period 16 (August 6, 2009).

8. The Education ?At-Post? Allowance rate changes are based on updated information provided by posts for the 2009-2010 school year.

9. The Education "Away-from-Post" Allowance rates are revised as shown below and are listed by COUNTRY. The school away-from-post rates are applicable for only (repeat ONLY) those posts within the listed countries where local schools are INADEQUATE in one or more grades as indicated in Section 920 of the Department of State Standardized Regulations (DSSR). To determine which grades are INADEQUATE, Section 920 will show higher away-from-post education allowance rates than the at-post education allowance rates. If the away-from-post education allowance rates
