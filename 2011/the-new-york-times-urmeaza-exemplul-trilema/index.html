The New York Times urmeaza exemplul Trilema

Thursday, 17 March, Year 3 d.Tr. | Author: Mircea Popescu

A Letter to Our Readers About Digital Subscriptions
Published: March 17, 2011

Today marks a significant transition for The New York Times as we introduce digital subscriptions. It’s an important step that we hope you will see as an investment in The Times, one that will strengthen our ability to provide high-quality journalism to readers around the world and on any platform. The change will primarily affect those who are heavy consumers of the content on our Web site and on mobile applications.

De aici. In limba romana,

O scrisoare catre cititorii nostri despre abonamentele digitale
Publicat : 17 Martie 2011

Ziua de astazi marcheaza o schimbare semnificativa la New York Times, intrucit introducem abonamentele digitale. E un pas important pe care speram ca il veti privi ca pe o investitie in Times, o investitie care va intari capacitatea noastra de-a aduce jurnalism de calitate in fata cititorilor din intreaga lume si de pe orice platforma. Modificarile ii vor afecta in principal pe cei care sunt mari consumatori de continut pe site si aplicatii mobile.

Le urez succes noilor veniti!
