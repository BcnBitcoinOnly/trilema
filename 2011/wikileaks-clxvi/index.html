STATE FOR EB/IFD/OIA, EUR/ERA, AND EUR/AGS

E.O. 12958: N/A TAGS: PREL, ECIN, ECON, ETRD, AU, EUN SUBJECT: AUSTRIANS SKEPTICAL AFTER 10 YEARS OF EU MEMBERSHIP

Summary
-------
1. A recent poll by the Austrian Association for European Politics (OGE) revealed that the Austrian public remains highly skeptical of the EU. A significant majority of Austrians do not believe EU membership has brought the benefits they expected, such as higher growth, lower inflation, and reduced crime rates. An OGE official concluded the overwhelming "yes" vote in the 1994 membership referendum represented a peak in pro-EU sentiment. In response to the OGE poll, the Austrian National Bank (ANB) characterized EU membership as positive from an economic standpoint. The OGE poll indicated that various factors contributed to the negative view: weak association with a European identity; a general distrust of Brussels-generated policies, which Austrian media and politicians often exploit; and lingering resentment at sanctions the EU imposed on Austria in 2000. End Summary.

Austrians Highly Skeptical of EU
--------------------------------
2. Ten years after accession, Austrians remain highly skeptical of the EU, according to a recent poll by the non-partisan Austrian Association for European Politics (OGE). OGE Secretary General Gerhard Bauer opined that the 67% vote for EU membership in 1994 was the peak of "EU-phoria" in Austria. Bauer noted Austrians' negative views of the EU were somewhat surprising given that Austria is a highly integrated EU member. Economic data confirms the benefits of EU membership, and no political party questions EU membership.

Poll Results - Perceptions Lag Behind Reality -- THE NEW POPE CONTINUES TO ELICIT INTEREST AND COMMENTARIES, WITH RIGHT-OF-CENTER LE FIGARO DEVOTING FIVE PAGES AND ITS EDITORIAL, TITLED, "BENEDICT THE EUROPEAN" TO HIS DOCTRINE AND WORLD REACTION TO HIS ELECTION.

THE EUROPEAN CONSTITUTION AND FRANCE'S REFERENDUM ARE ALSO A LEAD STORY, WITH POPULAR RIGHT-OF-CENTER LE PARISIEN HEADLINING: "YES, THE `YES' CAN STILL WIN." THE STORY IS LINKED TO A POLL CONDUCTED ON BEHALF OF THE PAPER BY THE CSA POLLING INSTITUTE AFTER PRESIDENT CHIRAC'S CONTROVERSIAL TELEVISED SHOW. IT INDICATES THAT ALTHOUGH THE `NO' IS STILL IN THE LEAD WITH 52 PERCENT, IT HAS LOST 4 PERCENTAGE POINTS, AND THE `YES,' GAINING 4 PERCENTAGE POINTS, IS UP TO 48 PERCENT.

SEVERAL COMMENTARIES ARE DEVOTED TO PRESIDENT CHIRAC'S ROLE IN TRIGGERING THIS NEGATIVE REACTION TO THE REFERENDUM. IN LE POINT, COLUMNIST NICOLAS BAVAREZ WRITES: "THE FEAR OF CHANGE EXPRESSED BY THE YOUNG PEOPLE (PARTICIPATING IN THE TELEVISED SHOW) IS A DIRECT PRODUCT OF CHIRAC'S DEMAGOGUERY. HE HAS TRAPPED HIMSELF; IN DENOUNCING THE CHIMERA OF AN `ULTRA- LIBERAL, ANGLO-SAXON AND ATLANTICIST EUROPE' HE HAS FED THE CAMPAIGN FOR THE `NO.' THE TRUTH IS THAT THERE IS A MAJORITY EXPRESSING ITSELF AGAINST CHIRAC, AND EUROPE HAS BECOME AN UNFORTUNATE COLLATERAL DAMAGE."

THE NEW ROMANIAN PRESIDENT, WHO IS QUITE UPFRONT ABOUT HIS ATTRACTION TO THE U.S., HAS ELICITED SOME ANNOYANCE FROM OTHER EU MEMBERS WITH HIS MENTION OF A `BUCHAREST-LONDON-WASHINGTON AXIS.' AFTER HAVING HELPED BUCHAREST IN ITS EFFORTS TO JOIN THE EU, FRANCE IS NOT HAPPY TO HAVE BEEN LEFT OUT IN THE COLD. FM BARNIER HAS ALREADY SAID THAT BASESCU DOES NOT PROVE TO HAVE A `EUROPEAN REFLEX' AND THAT HE CONFUSES `ALLIANCE' WITH `ALLEGIANCE' TO THE AMERICANS. THE ROMANIAN PRESIDENT HAS RESPONDED BY SAYING IN RECENT INTERVIEWS THAT HE `DOES NOT APPRECIATE RECEIVING SUCH LESSONS.' ON HIS VISIT TO PARIS ON TUESDAY THE ROMANIAN EU MINISTER WAS ASKED BY HIS FRENCH COUNTERPART TO DENY SUCH COMMENTS, WHICH HE IMMEDIATELY DID. WHAT LIES BEHIND THIS QUARREL? THE TRUTH IS THAT THE ROMANIAN PRESIDENT WANTS HIS COUNTRY TO BECOME A REGIONAL POWER AND IS COUNTING ON THE AMERICANS FOR THIS. THE COMPLEX GAME BETWEEN THE U.S. AND RUSSIA IS AN OPPORTUNITY FOR ROMANIA. AS A NATO MEMBER, IT IS READY TO WELCOME U.S. BASES. AND HAS OFFERED TO EXPAND NATO'S `ACTIVE ENDEAVOR' OPERATION TO THE BLACK SEA, WHICH FRANCE AND TURKEY OPPOSE. THE GUAM SUMMIT COULD HELP THAT DORMANT ORGANIZATION TO COME BACK TO LIFE THANKS TO THE AMERICANS, WHO, TO COUNTER RUSSIA'S INFLUENCE, WANT TO TURN THE GUAM INTO A SUB-REGIONAL COOPERATION ORGANIZATION FAVORABLE TO THE WEST. ROMANIA, AN OBSERVER AT THE SUMMIT, COULD FIND A ROLE TO FIT ITS AMBITIONS." WOLFF
