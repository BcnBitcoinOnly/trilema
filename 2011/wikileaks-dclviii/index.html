E.O. 12958: DECL: 02/28/2032 TAGS: PINR, PREL, PARM, ETTC, KOMC, IR, RO SUBJECT: ROMANIA STOPS MILSPEC LIQUID-OXYGEN GAS CONVERTERS HEADED TO TEHRAN

Classified By: Polcouns Ted Tanoue for Reasons 25X6 and 1.4(b)(d)(e).

1. (S) On February 27, Gicu Pop from the National Agency for Export Control (ANCEX) alerted Polmiloff to the disposition of five U.S.-origin, Liquid-Oxygen Gas Converter capsules en route to Iran, but held by Romanian Customs Authorities at Otopeni Airport. The Customs officials notified ANCEX when they identified a discrepancy between the Bill of Lading description of five "used empty medical oxygen capsules" and the markings on the capsules themselves that identified them as U.S. Property, Liquid Oxygen Gas Converters for Aircraft manufactured by the ARO Corporation, Bryan, Ohio. The containers also have seals with US Air Force insignia and the wording "Oklahoma City ALC" (e.g., the USAF Air Logistics Center at Tinker AFB). ANCEX and GOR Customs report the waybill, dated February 9, 2007 indicated no export license was required, but also that the items were exported from the U.S. in accordance with Export Administration regulations. For a shipment destined for Iran, this seemed highly irregular according to Pop. The waybill also carries a disclaimer that states: "Diversion contrary to U.S. law prohibited. Shipment may be carried via intermediate stopping places which DHL deems appropriate."

2. (S) Pop shared a fax received from Access USA on February 20, which said the goods were sent to the Romanian firm "General Service Trans SRL" located in Bucharest by mistake and the fax also requested the shipment to be forwarded to Azzizi Trading in Tehran. Pop reported that General Service Trans SRL is owned by a Romanian of Iranian nationality. Pop said because the documentation for the shipment was not in order, ANCEX was planning to instruct Customs to return the shipment to sender, Jameson Wilson Shipping of Bradenton, Florida. Polmiloff expressed appreciation for ANCEX alerting the embassy to this shipment.
