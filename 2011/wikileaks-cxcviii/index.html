E.O. 12958: N/A TAGS: PGOV, PHUM, SOCI, KDEM, ECON, RO, political assessment, poll SUBJECT: POLLS SHOW STRONG SUPPORT FOR BASESCU, CENTER- RIGHT ALLIANCE

REF: BUCHAREST 001084

1. Summary: Opinion polls conducted in May and June indicate continued strong support for President Traian Basescu, particularly after the release of three Romanian hostages from Iraq on May 22. A poll of June 14 showed Basescu with an approval rating of 71 percent while his center-right Liberal-Democratic (PNL-PD) Alliance also received high marks. Another major poll, conducted during the recently concluded hostage crisis, showed continued concern about corruption, and a lack of faith in the parliament and political parties. End Summary.

5. Comment: The results of these two recent polls confirm that Basescu and the PNL-PD Alliance still remain popular after six months in office. No doubt, Basescu's successful resolution in bringing home the three Romanian hostages from Iraq contributes to this continued buoyancy. Interestingly, Gallup also noted that this was the first time since 1997 that more respondents expressed belief that the country is headed in the right direction than not. Former FM Geoana's strong showing in the CURS poll followed his election as PSD president at the April 21 party conference. End Comment.
