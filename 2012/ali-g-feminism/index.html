Ali G - Feminism

Wednesday, 12 September, Year 4 d.Tr. | Author: Mircea Popescu

Ca pedeapsa pentru puletii ridicoli cu "hip hopu" am sa va transcriu cit si traduc dinjos niste beletristica.

Ali G : Booyakasha! Check dis! Today we iz tokin about wimen. I iz with non'oder den professa Soo Lis. She be darekta of da Senta fo Genda Resarch. And we iz goin to be tokin aba lediz. Now one in two people in da country iz a woman and that's why we got to know about em. Women, dey iz important aren't dey.

Soo Lis : They indeed are. Very important. As important as men.

Ali G : Which is betta ? Man or Woman ?

Soo Lis : Well equality is not about being better.

Ali G : But which one iz betta ?

Soo Lis : Ahaha to think... I ... neither is better.

Ali G : But one must be a little bit betta.

Soo Lis : In what respect ?

Ali G : Like... you know, in da way tha somfink is worse an somfink is betta

[cutout]

Ali G : Do you fink there will ever be a female Prime Minister ?

Soo Lis : Well there has been one.

Ali G : When ?!

Soo Lis : Margaret Thatcher.

Ali G : No she wasn't Prime Minister.

Soo Lis : Yes.

Ali G : She was da d... Defense Minister.

Soo Lis : No, she was Prime Minister.

Ali G : Do you think another woman will be allowed to slip forth ?

Soo Lis : The real question is whether there will be a president of the US who is a woman.

Ali G : Do you fink a woman should be allowed to have any job ?

Soo Lis : I do, yes. I think they should be able to have any job.

Ali G : Would you feel safe tho if you knew a woman was flying your plane ?

Soo Lis : Why wouldn't you feel safe if a woman were flying it ? Do you feel safe being driven by a woman ?

Ali G : Nop. Aren't you actually scared that she'd start not really thinking about things and then forget ta fly da plein ?

Soo Lis : That's incredible prejudice, you know!

Ali G : Whay ?

Soo Lis : Well, because there's no evidence that women... women are just as reliable and capable workers as men are.

[cutout]

Pe romaneste :

Ali G : Booyakasha! Fiti pe faza! Azi vorbim de femei. Sunt aici cu nimeni alta decit profesorul Soo Lis. Ea e directoru' de la centru' de relatii geniale, si vom vorbi de gagici. Noa acuma un om din doi in tara asta e femeie, si de aia trebuie sa aflam despre ele. Femeile is importante, gen ?

Soo Lis : Chiar sunt. Foarte importante. La fel de importante ca si barbatii.

Ali G : Ce-i mai bine ? Barbat sau femeie ?

Soo Lis : Pai egalitatea nu-i despre a fi mai bun.

Ali G : Dar care ii mai bun ?

Soo Lis : Hahah ce idee... pai... nimeni nu-i mai bun.

Ali G : Dar careva tre' sa fie un pic mai bun.

Soo Lis : In ce sens ?

Ali G : Gen... cum e faza cind ceva e mai naspa si ceva e mai bun ?

[taiat]

Ali G : Crezi ca va fi vre-odata o femeie Prim Ministru ?

Soo Lis : Pai a fost.

Ali G : Cind ?

Soo Lis : Margaret Thatcher.

Ali G : Nu ca n-a fost Prim Ministru.

Soo Lis : A fost.

Ali G : A fost Ministrul Apararii.

Soo Lis : Nu, a fost Prim Ministru.

Ali G : Crezi ca i se va mai permite altei femei sa se strecoare-n fata ?

Soo Lis : Chestiunea acum e daca va ajunge o femeie presedinte al SU.

Ali G : Crezi ca ar trebui sa i se permita unei femei sa aiba un loc de munca ?

Soo Lis : Cred ca da. Cred ca ar trebui sa poata avea orice loc de munca.

Ali G : Te-ai simti in siguranta la gindul ca o femeie iti piloteaza avionul ?

Soo Lis : Tu nu te-ai simti ? Te simti in siguranta cind o femeie iti conduce masina ?

Ali G : Nu. Nu ti-e teama ca va incepe sa nu se gindeasca la chestii si pe urma sa uite ca ea pilota avionu' ?

Soo Lis : Ce prejudecati incredibile, stii ?

Ali G : De ce ?

Soo Lis : Pai... pentru ca nu exista nici un fel de dovezi ca femeile... femeile sunt la fel de capabile ca si barbatii.

[taiat]

Noa asa. Interpretarea eventual pe youtube, cel putin pina cind o sterg jegosii.
