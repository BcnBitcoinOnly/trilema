N-am dormit, in limba engleza.

Thursday, 02 August, Year 4 d.Tr. | Author: Mircea Popescu

Asta noapte banuiesc eu ca voi iti fi dormit. Asa suspectez, daca nu de pe la zece seara si nici de pe la miezul noptii apai de la doua-trei incolo sigur ati impus perna cu cornu'. Eu in schimb nu.

Pentru ca m-am jucat. Uite asa cam de pe la doua noaptea, cind i-o rapus osteneala pe toti lesinind pe capete fiecare-n toate partile si avind astfel si eu liniste si pina pe la doua la prinz, cind i-o apucat pe aceleasi disperarea ca muare stapinul, aduceti chestii. Ora la care am imbucat peach cobbler facut special ca jertfa&ofranda zeului ce mi-s si-am decis planuitor asa ca pfoai, deci iata, am atins nivelu' 70 la Offensive Magic plus 60 la Heavy Armor Craft, sunt centaur, e cazul sa ma culc.

Am tras draperiile, am apasat butonu' de izolare fonica, am declarat Stare de Mers pe Virfuri in tata provincia (ca asa mi-o zis un cetitor, sa ne facem cutotii tara din proprietati, da' io cum am mai multe ma vaz silit sa-mi fac din fiecare cite-o provincie iliricoi-administrativa), mi-am luat in picioare sapcuta de vise placute si respectiv mantia de Stapinul Cosmarurilor pe umeri si-am purces a urca acest turn nesfirsit printre nori cum am putea alegoric reprezenta somnul la fel de bine ca oricum altcumva.

Ideea era ca voi dormi, mari, dormi, cam pina pe la zece dimineata zilei de Vineri, cind de altfel si urma sa fac chestii, planuite diverse. Asta se intimpla deci pe la trei ceasuri, sa fi fost. Pe la sapte si ceva am iesit din rezervatia in care urma sa-mi petrec tot restul zilei spre groaza & vaierele tuturor celor care stiu cum is io cind n-am dormit precum si cauzele pentru care incep razboaie in lume cit si lovesc molime. Si le-am declarat ritos ca "the difference between what you plan and what you happens is that what you plan is what you plan and what happens is what happens", care mi se pare o fraza foarte utila cit si descriptiva, productia mea proprie si originala da' n-am nimic impotriva daca ma citati. Ca-i adevarat. Sa v-o zic si-n engleza ?

Noa si daca tot suntem la discutia Evenimentelor Intimplatoare, sa va invrednicesc si cu o poza de mai demult :

Sa va transcriu partile mai interesante :

  • Sunday, August 1ii. Mobs arose in Southwark, Lambeth and Tyburn-Road and took it upon'em to interrogate People whether they were for the English or Irish? but committed no Violence ; several Parties of Horse Grenadiers dispers'd the Mobs which were gathering in Ratcliff-highway to demolish the Houses of the Irish.

  • Monday, 2. A very extraordinary Cause was try'd at Hertford Assizes, on an Action brought against the Defendant for debauching the Plaintiff's Daugher, (both Persons of Fortune,) and having a Child by her under Marriage Promises. A special Jury gave her 150 l. Damages, and directed her to bring an Action in her own Right upon a Marriage Contract.

  • Thursday, 5. A great Cause was tried at Chelmsford, Essex, between sir John Eylas, Bart. Plaintiff and John Smart, Gamekeeper to the Hon. Edward Carteret, Esq; Defendant. The action was brought for shooting 3 Hunting Dogs. The Defendant justified, that the Dogs being in pursuit of the Deer in his Master's Park, and very near killing some of them; he did not maliciously but for the Preservation of the said Deer shoot the Dogs ; which the Judge seemed to admit as Lawful ; but the Jury (being Gentlemen) in regard to the Game Laws brought in a Verdict for the Plaintiff, and a Guineeaiii and a half Damages. The Judge declared, that if a new Tryal was moved for, he would certify in Behalf of the Defendant.

  • Wednesday, 10iv. Four Malefactors were hang'd at Tyburn, Tho. Mills and S. Phillips, for Horsestealing ; John Maxwerth alias Parliament Jack for Burglary ; Johnv Kelsey for robbing the Cirencester Coach. Mills declar'd just before he was turn'd offvi that he was not guilty of the Fact. Rickets, Morris and Pritchard (See p. 422 A) were repreiv'd for 14 year's transportation.

  • Friday, 20. The Bakers at Dublin, not liking, the Assize of Bread made by the Lord Mayor, which was above 14 lb.vii Household Bread for 1 s.viii refused to bake 2 or 3 Days whereupon the Poor people in that City were in great Distress, some had Recourse to Potatoes, and some bought Flower to bake themselves, and found their Account in itix, tho' the Bakers thought their profit too smallx, but the Church Wardens threatened to present them as having no regular Visible Way of Living, on which they went to bake again.

  • Tuesday, 24. A fatter Boar was hardly ever seen than one taken up this Day coming out of Fleet Ditch into the Thames: It prov'd to be a Butcher's near Smithfield Bars, who had mist him 5 Months, all which Time, it seems, he had been in the common Sewer, and was improv'd in Price from 10 s. to 2 Guineas.

  • Wednesday, 25. Came on 2 remarkable Trials at Rochester, one of a Fellow for ravishing a Woman upwards of 60xi ; The other of a Soldier who pretended to cure a Boy of an Aguexii, and thinking to fright it away, by firing his Piece over the Boy's Head, levell'd it too low, and shot his Brains out. The Ravisher was castxiii, the Soldier acquitted.

Este ca-i informativa aceasta luna August de-acum niste ani ? Deci, cel mai mare porc (sa fi avut vre-un sfert de tona in viu ?) vine doua guinee, si deci la tona cam opt sau poate zece. Piinea vine sase kilograme si mai bine la shilling, si deci tona cam opt sau noua guinee. Si apropo de care chestie, astazi piinea e cam zece lei kilogramul, si carnea de porc in viu cam tot atita - va dati seama minune, trei sute de ani mai tirziu acest echilibru fundamental inca subzista ?

Zece lei vin aproape doua lire sterline. Pe de cealalta parte, lira sterlina (sovereign) la momentul discutiei se batea din 113 grains de aur de 22 de carate, ceea ce vine 7,3 grame sau cam pe-acolo, si-ar valora azi cam doua sute de lire, sau vre-o mie de lei. Diferenta intre o mie de lei de azi si zece lei de-atunci se poate considera egala cu bunurile, serviciile si valorile adaugate de spiritul neastimparat al omului in decursul veacurilor, si-o putem privi foarte antropologic asa : din viata omului de azi viata omului de-atunci reprezinta un leu dintr-o suta. Restul, Internet, autobuze, nudism, filosofie, serii Taylor, triclosan si asa mai departe sunt adaugiri recente. Sau privind altfel problema, un an trait azi valoreaza cam o suta de ani traiti in 1736, cel putin sub raportul complexitatii si rafinamentului. Cum va pare ?

Un soldat, probabil beat, care-a impuscat in cap un copchil e bineinteles eliberat. Un tip care-a violat o baba e bineinteles spinzurat. Va poate soca azi acest "bineinteles", dar el isi are rostul lui : pe cind lumea era de o suta de ori mai simpla si viata omului era de o suta de ori mai valoroasa, si ca atare reaua intentie, acea celebra mens rea chiar mai conta la ceva. Astazi fapte si nu vorbe, oamenii nu mai intereseaza pe nimeni dincolo de suprafata lor dat fiind ca n-avem vreme si nici interes de-asa ceva. Ca nu este timp, tre' sa administram realitati virtuale si alte chestii. (S-ar putea spune ca traim intr-o lume de vis ? Ar spune oare englezul de-atunci adus azi ca e o lume de vis ?)

Neplacerile cauzate de-un tip bogat unei tipe bogate prin aia ca ii place s-o futa da' nu doreste sa se insoare costa 150 de lire, din care la nevoie s-ar cumpara cam o suta de scroafe tinere (in romaneste nu exista cuvint echivalent junincii pentru a descrie scroafa neinceputa, da' in englezeste-i zice yelt). Pe de alta parte trei ciini de vinatoare impuscati pe buna dreptate costa o lira juma', adica a suta parte (deci tipul isi putea lua la fel de bine si catele, la o adica). E vizibila acolo daca aveti ochi sa priviti tensiunea intre sistemul feudal deja mort si de jumatate ingropat si noul capitalism, acest sistem in care niste tipi fara merite si fara alte recomandari pun incet-incet mina pe toate institutiile statului, incalcind fara nici o retinere si legile si bunul simt din simpla apartenenta de casta.

Luna August e in general una dintre cele mai reusite luni ale anului. Alaturi, bineinteles, de celelalte unsprezece.
