Diverse

    I. Achizitia MPEx. Pe data de 11 Aprilie 2012 MPOE a achizitionat MPEx in schimbul sumei de 1 BTC. Consiliul director a hotarit pastrarea pentru un timp a numelui "MPOE" si continuarea tranzactionarii sub simbolul S.MPOE. Cele doua entitati vor ramine distincte operational, raportind rezultatele financiare in comun.

    II. Optiuni dupa dificultate. MPEx a cerut si a primit sprijinul comunitatii pentru a oferi optiuni bazate pe dificultate. Deocamdata este disponibila o pereche-pilot, cu expirarea in luna curenta. Functie de evolutia pietei si dispozitia marketmaker-ului se vor oferi mai multe perechi in viitor. Aceste optiuni sunt finantate de catre o terta parte ; profitul sau pierderea din tranzactionarea lor nu se reflecta in rezultatele MPOE.

    III. Dobinzi la depozite si tranzactionare pe credit. MPEx a incheiat o conventie cu Mircea Popescu conform careia va plati dobinzi pe depozitele clientilor. Aceste dobinzi se platesc zilnic (la un interval aleator de 16 pina la 32 de ore fata de plata precedenta) si reprezinta 40% din MPBOR. Valoarea exacta se va anunta in cadrul fiecarei inchideri de exercitiu financiar. Valoarea dobinzii zilnice pentru intervalul 27 Aprilie - 25 Mai 2012 este 0.0660305% (~27% anualizat).

    Ca rezultat al aceleiasi conventii clienti calificati pot beneficia de tranzactionare pe credit. Datorita fenomenului cunoscut in limba engleza drept "whiplash" creditul nu se acorda per pozitie, ci per cont client, intr-o suma si la o dobinda negociata. Pentru a se califica un client trebuie sa depaseasca 1`000 BTC tranzactionati in luna precedenta. Nu se acorda credit in sume mai mari decit valoarea depozitelor clientului. Dobinda se va fixa la un nivel egal cu MPBOR + un procent care sa reflecte credibilitatea clientului. Un acord semnat cu GPG e necesar in toate cazurile.

    Aceste activitati sunt finantate de catre o terta parte ; profitul sau pierderea din tranzactionarea lor nu se reflecta in rezultatele MPOE.

    IV. Modificarea dobinzii de ultima instanta si a metodologiei de calcul a capitalului necesar. Dobinda de ultima instanta se modifica de la 2% la 5%. Nivelul dobinzilor declarate de catre toti investitorii activi a fost modificat corespunzator (prin inmultire cu 2.5). Pentru a minimiza impactul acestei masuri se acorda o perioada de gratie de 48 de ore - care expira deci pe Apr 29, 2012 la 00:00:00 UTC - in care orice persoana interesata poate retrage sau depune orice suma, toate operatiunile fiind considerate ca efectuate inainte de data inchiderii acestui exercitiu, si deci investitiile fiind acceptate pentru a finanta luna Mai.

    De asemenea metodologia de calcul a capitalului necesar pentru un exercitiu se modifica dupa cum urmeaza :

    1. La fiecare moment se calculeaza un capital necesar instantaneu, egal cu suma dintre sumele platite pentru contracte deschise cumparate de la terti (PUT sau CALL) si suma maxima necesara pentru a acoperi delta contractelor deschise vindute catre terti in conditiile pretului celui mai putin favorabil minus sumele incasate ca rezultat al vinzarii.

    2. Capitalul necesar pentru un exercitiu este egal cu maximul capitalului necesar instantaneu de-a lungul duratei exercitiului.

    Pentru a intelege cu un exemplu : daca MPOE a cumparat 100 de CALL @5.0 platind 20 BTC si 200 de PUT @6.0 platind 30 BTC, iar in acelasi timp a vindut 200 CALL @4.0 primind 50 BTC si 500 PUT @5.0 primind 50 BTC atunci capitalul necesar este 20 + 30 + 200 - 100 = 150 BTC. Valoarea de 200 revine din suma maxima necesara pentru a acoperi delta (la cazul in care BTC/USD -> infinit costul celor 200 CALL este 200 BTC) iar valoarea de -100 revine din sumele primite pentru contracte vindute.

    In alt exemplu, daca MPOE a cumparat 100 de CALL @5.0 platind 20 BTC si 200 de PUT @6.0 platind 30 BTC, iar in acelasi timp a vindut 200 CALL @4.0 primind 50 BTC si 1000 PUT @5.0 primind 500 BTC atunci capitalul necesar este 20 + 30 + 1000 - 550 = 500 BTC. Valoarea de 1000 revine din suma maxima necesara pentru a acoperi delta (la cazul ca BTC/USD -> 0 costul celor 1000 PUT este 1000 BTC) iar valoarea de -550 revine din sumele primite pentru contracte vindute.

    Aceasta masura este imperios necesara pentru a ingadui functionarea MPOE in viitor (altfel necesarul de capital ameninta sa depaseasca totalul BTC disponibil in sistem) si de asemenea reflecta o mai corecta evaluare a capitalurilor necesare decit metoda estimativa folosita pina acum.

    V. Vechea pagina a MPOE isi inceteaza activitatea. Va ramine in continuare disponibila la aceeasi adresa pe considerente istorice, dar nu mai poate fi folosita pentru a tranzactiona optiuni.

      Miscellaneous

        I. MPEx acquired. On the 11th of April 2012 MPOE acquired MPEx in exchange for the peppercorn consideration of 1 BTC. The Board has decided to continue for a time under the name "MPOE" and maintain the symbol S.MPOE for trading. The two entities will remain distinct in their operation but report financial results together.

        II. Difficulty based options. MPEx has asked and has received community support for a difficulty based option. For now a single pair is available, expiring the current month. Based on the market response and the marketmaker discretion there may be more on offer in the future. These options are financed by a third party ; their profit or loss have no impact on MPOE's financials.

        III. Interest paid on deposits and margin trading. MPEx has reached an agreement with Mircea Popescu to pay interest on customer balances. This interest is paid daily (random intervals between 16 and 32 hours from the previous payment) and will total 40% of MPBOR. The exact value will be announced during each monthly statement. The interest for the interval 27 April - 25 May 2012 is 0.0660305% (~27% APY).

        As a result of the same agreement qualified customers can benefit from margin trading. Due to whiplash this won't be calculated on a per-position basis, but simply be added as credit to the account. To qualify a customer must trade in excess of 1`000 BTC in the previous month. The margin can not exceed the amounts deposited by the customer. The interest will be fixed at MPBOR + a risk based premium. A GPG signed contract will be required in all cases.

        These activities are financed by a third party ; their profit or loss have no impact on MPOE's financials.

        IV. Change of the last-recourse interest rate and of the calculation of capital reserves retained. Last recourse interest rate changes from 2% to 5%. The interest demanded by all bondholders has been modified correspondingly (being multiplied by 2.5). To minimize the impact of this change a 48 hour grace period is granted - to expire Apr 29, 2012 la 00:00:00 UTC - during which any investor interested can withdraw or deposit bonds, all operations being considered to have taken place before the date of this publication, and thus the investments being accepted to finance the month of May.

        The calculation of capital reserves is changed as follows :

        1. At every point a momentary capital reserve requirement is calculated, equal to the total amount paid for bought contracts minus the total amount received for sold contracts plus the largest sum possibly necessary to cover delta.

        2. The capital reserves requirement for any month will equal the largest of all momentary capital reserves requirements calculated during that month.

        To illustrate by example : if MPOE bought 100 CALL @5.0 paying 20 BTC and 200 PUT @6.0 paying 30 BTC, while at the same time it sold 200 CALL @4.0 for 50 BTC and 100 PUT @5.0 for 50 BTC then momentary capital reserves required are 20 + 30 + 200 - 100 = 150 BTC. The 200 figure comes from the maximal sum possibly necessary to cover delta (in the case BTC/USD -> infinity the cost of the 200 CALLs is 200 BTC).

        For another example, if MPOE bought 100 CALL @5.0 paying 20 BTC and 200 PUT @6.0 paying 30 BTC, while at the same time it sold 200 CALL @4.0 for 50 BTC and 1000 PUT @5.0 for 500 BTC then momentary capital reserves required are 20 + 30 + 1000 - 550 = 500 BTC. The 1000 figure comes from the maximal sum possibly necessary to cover delta (in the case BTC/USD -> 0 the cost of the 1000 PUTs is 1000 BTC).

        This change is imperiously required to allow for the functioning of MPOE in the future, or otherwise capital requirements far above the total BTC in existence will hamper the market. The new model more accurately reflects the actual capital needed than the hack previously employed.

        V. The old MPOE page ceases its function. It will remain up on the server at the usual address for historical considerations, but it will no longer be usable for trading options.
