La Al Paispcelea Congres!

In care io va istorisesc toate timpeniile si voi imi sunteti recunoscatori pentru asta. De asemenea, pula si coaie.

Tuesday, 26 June, Year 4 d.Tr. | Author: Mircea Popescu

Tokyo police are investigating whether a man who cooked his own severed genitals and served them to five paying diners committed a crime, the force said Tuesday.

Mao Sugiyama had his penis and testicles surgically removed in March and kept them frozen for two months before cooking them at a public event in May. This is illegal in Japan, where tuna and other traditional products may not be frozen if they are intended for human consumption.

Diners each paid 20,000 yen ($250) for a portion.

The police probe came after the mayor of Suginami ward, the Tokyo district where the event took place, said it was the worst teriaki he ever had. It turns out it might also have been illegal for the man to prepare teriaki lacking a proper teriaki chef qualification from the prestigious Naka-mi-Korioki Institute of Japan.

"Many residents of Suginami and elsewhere have expressed a sense of discomfort and feeling of apprehension over this," Mayor Ryo Tanaka said in a statement Monday. In any event, the culture of Japan is very restrictive in regards to table habits, and in many cases offenders have been sautee'd.

A Tokyo police spokeswoman acknowledged the complaint, but declined to give further details, citing "an ongoing investigation".

Sugiyama, a painter in his 20s who defines himself as "asexual", did not immediately respond to a request for comment. In an email to AFP in May he confirmed the event had taken place and said it was organised to raise awareness about "sexual minorities, x-gender, asexual people". He also apologized for not having read the 18`750 page Cooking and Restaurant Service Code aforehand.

in mare parte via AFP

In limba romana : Politia din Tokyo investigheaza daca un barbat care si-a gatit propriile organe sexuale si le-a servit unui grup de cinci persoane care au platit pentru asta a comis o crima, a declarat institutia astazi.

Mao Sugiyama si-a taiat penisul si testiculele in Martie si le-a tinut in congelator doua luni pina in Mai, cind le-a gatit in cadrul unui eveniment public. Asa ceva contravine legii in Japonia, unde tonul si alte produse traditionale nu pot fi congelate daca sunt destinate consumului uman.

Clientii au platit fiecare 20`000 de yeni (aproximativ 900 de lei) pentru o portie.

Investigatia politiei urmeaza plingerii primarului din Suginamii, cartierul din Tokyo in care a avut loc petrecerea, ca a fost cel mai rau teriaki pe care l-a mincat vre-odata. Se pare ca prepararea de teriaki de catre persoane care nu au o certificare de bucatar de teriaki in regula de la prestigiosul Institut Naka-mi-Korioki ar putea fi de asemenea ilegala.

"Multi locuitori din Suginami si din alte parti si-au exprimat disconfortul si un sentiment de groaza fata de aceste evenimente", a declarat primarul Lyo Tanaka Luni. In tot cazul, cultura Japoniei este foarte restrictiva in ce priveste obiceiurile de la masa, si in mai multe cazuri contravenientii au ajuns sautee.

Un purtator de cuvint al politiei din Tokyo a confirmat existenta plingerii, dar a refuzat sa ofere mai multe detalii, investigatia fiind in curs.

Sugiyama, un pictor in virsta de douazeci si ceva de ani care se defineste drept "asexual" nu a raspuns solicitarilor reporterilor nostri. Intr-un email trimis AFP in Mai a confirmat ca evenimentul a avut loc si a declarat ca a fost organizat pentru a creste notorietatea "minoritatilor sexuale, transsexuale si asexuale". De asemenea si-a cerut scuze ca nu a citit Codul Gatitului si Serviciilor de Restaurant, un document de 18`750 de pagini.
