Supunem arbitrajului public o problema neimportanta

Friday, 20 January, Year 4 d.Tr. | Author: Mircea Popescu
Eu : so lorena lupu wrote big piece about the importance of work
Ea : haha okay

Eu : i put it into fain saying i like her a lot on this point. it's something to state the importance of work even tho it's never going to help you.
Ea : ahh, the moans of the hall of the hateds

Eu : lol ?
Ea : you has a hall
Ea : filled with people

Eu : i do ?
Ea : that you has decided to hate yes
Ea : and they can do no good!
Ea : and they will be mocked!

Eu : but i just said!
Ea : at random and forever!
Ea : and they moan!
Ea : it is a mock!

Eu : i was serious
Ea : do you think we do not detect mock?
Ea : grr.

Eu : srsly. i think my hall mostly exist cause teh guilty misdetect mock
Ea : why are you being all difficult?
Ea : even tho it's never going to help you.
Ea : THAT'S the mock part.

Eu : it's not mock.
Eu : its statement of sad fact.
Eu : she's an untalented boot.
Ea : sigh.

Eu : what sigh!
Ea : mock/mäk/Verb: Tease or laugh at in a scornful or contemptuous manner.
Ea : even if you're serious
Ea : it still in fact mocks her.

Eu : did not scorn nor contempt.
Ea : look

Eu : no looky.
Ea : you rrrrr

Eu : a blind dog praises the beauty of paintings
Ea : ::spaz::

Eu : and i say it's a great thing for a dog to praise the beauty of painting
Eu : even if it's never going to do anything for him.
Eu : tis tragic not comedic.
Ea : this issue whereby your judgement has to be taken literally as absolute truth is getting in the way of basic conversational understanding.

Eu : lmao. why ?
Ea : because you've decided the woman is useless as a worker, and while that can be taken literally and entirely honestly seriously and therefore your statement isnt mockful, the vast majority of people will see that this is a judgement against her that is KIND OF FUNNY rather than her actual state as ruled by god.

Eu : yes well. people are clueless.

Eu : mdeci lorena lupu o scris ditai textul despre importanta muncii
Ea : haha okay

Eu : pe care-am pus-o pe fain zicind ca-mi place foarte mult la faza asta, e ceva sa enunti importanta muncii chiar daca nu-ti va ajuta la nimic.
Ea : ahh, gemetele din sala celor uriti.

Eu : lol ?
Ea : ai o sala
Ea : plina cu oameni

Eu : am o sala ?
Ea : pe care te-ai hotarit sa-i urasti da.
Ea : indiferent ce-ar face!
Ea : vor fi batjocoriti!

Eu : dar dimpotriva am zis!
Ea : aleator pentru totdeauna!
Ea : si gem!
Ea : e o batjocura!

Eu : vorbeam serios.
Ea : crezi ca nu detectam batjocura ?
Ea : grr.

Eu : pe bune. cre' ca sala exista in principal pentru ca vinovatii detecteaza gresit batjocura.
Ea : de ce esti atita de dificil!
Ea : chiar daca nu-ti va ajuta la nimic
Ea : AIA e batjocura.

Eu : nu-i batjocura.
Eu : e pur si simplu enuntul unei triste stari de fapt.
Eu : fata-i o cizma netalentata.
Ea : of.

Eu : ce of!
Ea : mock/mäk/Verb: Tease or laugh at in a scornful or contemptuous manner.
Ea : chiar daca vorbesti serios
Ea : tot o batjocoreste.

Eu : nici dispret nici batjocura!
Ea : uite!

Eu : nu, uite!
Ea : tu rrrrr

Eu : un ciine orb lauda frumusetea picturilor
Ea : ::spasme::

Eu : si eu zic ca-i o mare chestie ca un ciine sa laude frumusetea picturii
Eu : chiar daca n-o sa-i serveasca niciodata.
Eu : e tragic nu comic.
Ea : faza asta ca judecatile tale trebuie luate literalmente drept adevaruri absolute incepe sa interfereze cu intelegerea elementara a conversatiei.

Eu : lmao. de ce ?
Ea : pentru ca ai decis ca femeia munceste degeaba, si chiar daca asta se poate lua literal si complet serios asa incit ce-ai zis sa nu fie o batjocura, totusi vasta majoritate a oamenilor vor considera ca-i vorba de-o judecata de valoare care-i AMUZANTA mai degraba decit de starea ei reala asa cum a fost enuntata de divinitate.

Eu : apai noa. oamenii n-au habar.

Mnoa, nu mai retin ce anume se supune arbitrajului public, da' voi raspundeti oricum.
